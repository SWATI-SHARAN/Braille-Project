How It Works

SpeakDots uses advanced Deep Learning and Computer Vision to bridge the communication gap.

1

Image Preprocessing

When you upload an image, our system first cleans it. We apply noise reduction, thresholding, and contour detection to isolate the individual Braille dots from the paper texture.

2

Character Segmentation

The system identifies each Braille cell (a 2×3 grid of dots). It slices the image into individual character blocks, preparing them for the recognition engine.

3

CNN Recognition

A Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) trained on thousands of Braille samples analyses each cell. It predicts the corresponding English letter with high accuracy.

4

Text Reconstruction

The recognised characters are stitched back together into words and sentences. Finally, our Text-to-Speech engine converts this text into spoken audio.

Accessibility Statement

SpeakDots is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Our Accessibility Features

  • ⌨️

    Keyboard Navigation

    All interactive elements are focusable and navigable using the Tab key, ensuring users who cannot use a mouse can still operate the platform effectively.

  • 🎨

    High Contrast & Readability

    We strictly adhere to WCAG AA standards for color contrast. Our typography is chosen for maximum legibility, with scalable text sizes.

  • 🔊

    Screen Reader Compatibility

    The site structure uses semantic HTML5 landmarks (nav, main, footer) and ARIA labels where necessary to support screen reading software.
